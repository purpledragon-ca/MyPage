Our Story

From a chance meeting in Vancouver to building a life together, our journey has been painted in golden sunshine and soft purple moments—and now we get to celebrate with you.

First Meeting in Vancouver

January 2024 brought us together at an orientation session. Among the crowd, we found each other—two souls who didn't know yet how perfectly their paths would align.

Growing Together

We became each other's light and warmth. Zilong helped Lei heal from dark times, bringing comfort and hope. Lei opened new perspectives for Zilong, showing him different ways to see the world.

Shining in Each Other's Eyes

We're just ordinary people, but to each other, we shine bright. We share values, love fishing and outdoor adventures together, and found our ideal partner in one another. So we decided to make it forever.

Building Our Future

We cook delicious meals, explore Greater Vancouver, volunteer together, and build our home side by side. Here's to a lifetime of happiness, laughter, and love—forever and always.
